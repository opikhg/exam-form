Note

You must enter the testing room with your admission ticket and ID card (such as a government-issued resident registration card with a photo attached) before the designated testing time.

The photo must be a color photograph of your upper body, taken no more than six months before your application, without a hat and without a background. The photo must be 3.5cm wide and 4.5cm long. Photos that have been artificially composited or edited using photo editing software, filters, or AI are not permitted.

The driver's license application form (admission ticket) is valid for one year from the date of the initial written test, and the skills test must be passed within one year of the date of passing the written test.

If you obtain your driver's license through fraudulent means, your license will be revoked and you will be prohibited from taking the test for two years.

If you cheat on the driver's license test, your test will be invalidated and you will be prohibited from taking the test for two years from the date of the decision.

Passing the test during the disqualification period (6 months to 5 years) will be invalidated.

You can change your test date by visiting the testing center (before the test ends) or by contacting the Customer Center (up to one hour before the test begins).

When using the Customer Center (☎ 1577-1120), applicants taking the first period may change their test date by 6:00 PM the day before. (For applicants taking the first period on Monday, changes may be made by 6:00 PM on Friday (or the day before a public holiday).) ※ Changes can also be made on the Korea Road Traffic Authority's Integrated Safety Driving Civil Service website.

If you are applying for Class 1 or Class 2 regular driver's license, you must carry the provisional license attached to your admission ticket with you during your on-road driving practice.

Those who fail the on-site skills test or the on-road driving test may retake the test after at least three days from the date of failing the test.

Those who pass the on-site skills test (Class 1 Large/Special/Class 2 Small/Motorized) and the on-road driving test (Class 1 Regular/Class 2 Regular) must receive their driver's license within 30 days.

Fees: The amount determined and announced by the Korea Road Traffic Authority with the approval of the Commissioner of the National Police Agency in accordance with Article 139 (2) of the Road Traffic Act.
